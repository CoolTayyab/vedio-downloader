How to use Tayyab's Downloader

Four quick steps. No account needed.

1

Copy the link

Open the video in its app or website and copy the link from the address bar or the Share button.

2

Paste it here

Paste it in the link box. A preview with the title and thumbnail appears so you know it's the right video.

3

Pick your options

Choose MP4 video, MP3 audio or subtitles, then the quality and subtitle language you want.

4

Download

Press Download and watch the live progress. The file is saved to your device's Downloads folder.
